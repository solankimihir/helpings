Helpings is a calm, simple meal log. Write down what you ate in about ten seconds, see today's calories at a glance, and look back on any day — without accounts, ads or guesswork.

EXACT, NEVER ESTIMATED
Calories come only from what you enter. If a line has no calorie value, Helpings tells you the total is incomplete instead of making up a number.

FAST LOGGING
• Pick breakfast, lunch, dinner, snack or other — the time of day is pre-selected
• Search your own food list; quantity, unit and calories pre-fill from your defaults
• Not on the list? Create a new food right from the search
• Add a note to any meal

YOUR FOOD LIST
• Starts with everyday foods like roti, dal, rice, idli, poha and tea
• Set default calories and units (piece, gram, spoon, cup)
• Hide foods you no longer eat, or delete them — past meals keep their names
• Changing a default never rewrites meals you already saved

HISTORY
• Meals grouped by day with daily totals
• Filter by meal type
• Swipe to delete, tap to view or edit

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything stays on your phone. Helpings has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access at all.

Light and dark mode follow your system setting.
