Helpings is a calm, simple meal log. Log what you ate quickly and easily, see today's calories at a glance, and look back on any day — without accounts, ads or guesswork.

EXACT, NEVER ESTIMATED
Calories come only from what you enter. If a line has no calorie value, Helpings tells you the total is incomplete instead of making up a number.

FAST LOGGING
• The meal type is pre-selected from the time of day
• Search your own food list; quantity, unit and calories pre-fill from your defaults
• Not on the list? Create a new food right from the search
• Save foods you eat together as a combo, then add them in one tap
• Repeat yesterday's meal, or "Log again" any past meal
• Add a note to any meal

YOUR MEALS, YOUR WAY
• Breakfast, lunch, dinner and snack to start — rename, reorder or hide them, and add your own (pre-workout, brunch…)
• Optional reminders: a gentle nudge if a meal isn't logged by the time you choose
• Two home-screen widgets: today's calories with meal and item counts, and today's meals with every food in them. Both start fresh at midnight

OPTIONAL HEALTH TRACKING
• Turn on water, blood sugar, weight or period tracking in Settings and a Health tab appears. Leave them off and nothing changes
• Water: quick-add a glass, or any amount, against an optional daily goal
• Blood sugar: log readings with when you took them (fasting, before or after a meal, bedtime) and see the last two weeks
• Weight: weigh-ins with your 7 and 30 day change and a trend line
• NEW: Period: tap days on a calendar to log them, see your cycle day, average cycle and when your next period is likely
• mg/dL or mmol/L, kg or lb, ml or fl oz

YOUR FOOD LIST
• Starts with everyday foods like roti, dal, rice, idli, poha and tea
• Set default calories and units (piece, gram, spoon, cup)
• Hide foods you no longer eat, or delete them — past meals keep their names
• Give a food calories later and Helpings offers to fill in past meals that had none; nothing changes without asking

ASK AN AI
• Build a starter food list: copy a prompt into ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini. It asks where you live, which cuisines you eat, veg or non-veg, home-cooked or eating out, then suggests foods with calories
• Fill in missing calories: send your food list; the chat asks about portions and cooking, one question at a time, then fills in only the blanks
• Paste the reply back. Helpings backs up all your data first, and you review every change before anything is imported
• It's all copy and paste: Helpings itself still never goes online

HISTORY AND TRENDS
• Swipe between days on the home screen, or jump to any date
• Meals grouped by day with daily totals, filtered by meal type
• Weekly and monthly trends: calories per day, your daily average and most-logged foods
• Through the day. See your calories add up meal by meal, against yesterday, the same day last week or your 7-day average. Tap or drag to compare at any time

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access at all
• Save a backup copy whenever you like, or keep daily backups in a folder you choose (the last 30 are kept)
• Restore from any backup file
• Export your meals or your food list as a spreadsheet (CSV)

Light, dark or follow your system setting.
