Helpings is a calm, simple meal log. Write down what you ate in about ten seconds, see today's calories at a glance, and look back on any day — without accounts, ads or guesswork.

EXACT, NEVER ESTIMATED
Calories come only from what you enter. If a line has no calorie value, Helpings tells you the total is incomplete instead of making up a number.

FAST LOGGING
• The meal type is pre-selected from the time of day
• Search your own food list; quantity, unit and calories pre-fill from your defaults
• Not on the list? Create a new food right from the search
• Save foods you eat together as a combo, then add them in one tap
• Repeat yesterday's meal, or "Log again" any past meal
• Add a note to any meal

YOUR MEALS, YOUR WAY
• Breakfast, lunch, dinner and snack to start — rename, reorder or hide them, and add your own (pre-workout, brunch…)
• Optional reminders: a gentle nudge if a meal isn't logged by the time you choose
• Home-screen widget with today's calories and a quick Add meal button

YOUR FOOD LIST
• Starts with everyday foods like roti, dal, rice, idli, poha and tea
• Set default calories and units (piece, gram, spoon, cup)
• Hide foods you no longer eat, or delete them — past meals keep their names
• Changing a default never rewrites meals you already saved

HISTORY AND TRENDS
• Swipe between days on the home screen, or jump to any date
• Meals grouped by day with daily totals, filtered by meal type
• Weekly and monthly trends: calories per day, your daily average and most-logged foods

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access at all
• Save a backup copy whenever you like, or keep daily backups in a folder you choose (the last 30 are kept)
• Restore from any backup file
• Export all your meals as a spreadsheet (CSV)

Light, dark or follow your system setting.
